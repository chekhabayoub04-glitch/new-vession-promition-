Frequently asked questions

Is Hotel Youcef in Khenchela?

Yes. Hotel Youcef is in Khenchela Province, in the Aurès region; directions are on the map on this page.

Is there a pool?

Yes, the hotel has an outdoor pool for its guests.

Does the hotel have a banquet hall?

Yes. A single hall hosts both conferences and banquets for 120–135 pax, alongside a VIP meeting lounge for 17–20 pax. Request a quote from the Venues page.

How do I book?

Email our reservations team at reservation.hotelyoucef@gmail.com or call reception on 030 56 43 40. The hotel confirms your booking when it replies.

Is breakfast included in the price?

Yes. Every room and suite rate includes breakfast and taxes, according to the hotel's official price list.

A four-star hotel in Khenchela Province, in the heart of the Aurès: 84 rooms and suites, a pool, restaurant and café, a conference hall and a VIP lounge.
